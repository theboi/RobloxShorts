# In Pencil

Draft 1 — awaiting writing feedback. Complete standalone reel.

Target emotion: unease → recognition → relief and belonging.
Target length: approximately 65–75 seconds; timing is provisional until a voiced read.

## Voiceover and dialogue

Everyone in this house was written in pen.

Except me.

The kitchen doorway had years of little lines.

Ella at six. Ben at nine.

Mine said “Jamie. First week.”

In pencil.

I'd lived with three families before this one.

I knew why you used something you could erase.

So I kept my suitcase under the bed.

Never put stickers on anything.

And every time they measured me, I handed Dad the pencil.

On Saturday, Ella caught me rubbing out my first line.

“What are you doing?”

“Making room.”

She looked at the empty space.

Then she went and got Dad.

I started apologizing before he reached the kitchen.

He didn't say anything.

Just stood against the doorway.

“Measure me.”

I reached for the pencil.

He held out a marker.

“I'm pretty sure I'm done growing.”

I laughed. A little.

Then he stepped aside.

“Your turn?”

I stood against the wood.

He drew the line.

Then handed me the marker to write my name.

That night, I put a sticker on my bed.

## Visual beats

| Beat | Picture and performance |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | Tight vertical frame: older height marks in ink, Jamie's in faint pencil. Jamie's hand hovers beside his name. |
| History | One restrained insert of a worn suitcase with old luggage labels. Keep Jamie's previous families off screen; no villain montage. |
| Holding back | Suitcase tucked under bed; unopened sticker sheet inside. Jamie places his folded clothes back in it. |
| Repetition | Two quick height checks on different days; Jamie offers the pencil before Dad can choose a tool. This establishes that the pencil is Jamie's choice. |
| Erasing | Jamie rubs at the lowest mark. Ella notices and asks quietly. Her expression changes after his answer. |
| Dad arrives | Jamie lowers his eyes and starts apologizing. Dad takes in the erased patch, then puts his own back against the frame. |
| Gentle joke | Jamie measures Dad. Dad offers the marker with an ordinary, warm delivery; allow a brief genuine laugh. |
| Choice | Dad steps away and waits. Jamie chooses to stand at the frame. Keep the marker handoff visible. |
| Name | Jamie carefully writes his own name beside the fresh ink line. No adoption paperwork or claim that placement status has changed. |
| Ending | Back in the bedroom, Jamie takes one sticker from the sheet and presses it onto his bedframe. Hold on his thumb smoothing its edge; cut without a spoken moral. |

## Direction

- Three speaking characters; one kitchen doorway and one bedroom. Standard Roblox
  rigs, domestic props, and subtle performances; no custom fantasy mechanic.
- Jamie narrates conversationally. Leave a pause after “Making room” and before
  “Your turn?” Avoid tearful delivery throughout; the restraint makes the release work.
- Dad offers a choice rather than a promise he cannot guarantee. Ella brings him
  because she recognizes Jamie's distress, not because Jamie has done something wrong.
- Keep the pencil, marker, sticker sheet, and suitcase consistent between shots.
- Let the eraser and marker sounds register. Introduce warm music gently after
  the joke; do not overwhelm the final small action.
- If the voiced read runs long, trim the history and repetition before cutting
  the pauses around the invitation. This story should conclude in one reel.
