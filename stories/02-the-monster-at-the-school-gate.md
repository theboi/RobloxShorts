# The Monster at the School Gate

Production draft 1. Current shot timeline: 2:44. Complete episode; mild horror, no gore.
Cast: Nia (narrator), Sam and Bea (friends), Principal Sol, Bristle (guardian).
Creature contract: Bristle detects nonhuman organisms and restrains them. The cape
controls Sol while spread; pinning its fabric interrupts that control temporarily.

## Script

### 0:00–0:15 — The new guard

Our school hired a monster to keep monsters out.

It was taller than the gate.

Six eyes. Hands like shovels.

On Monday, I watched it sniff twelve students, a teacher, and a cheese sandwich.

It ate the sandwich.

Then it let everybody through.

### 0:15–0:30 — One person fails

On Friday, Principal Sol landed in the courtyard.

The monster screamed.

It leapt the gate and dragged him to the ground.

Teachers pulled it away.

Sol stood up smiling.

“Faulty equipment,” he said. “Lock it in the gym.”

### 0:30–0:47 — The wrong target

Sam said he'd seen its claws go for Sol's throat.

But Bea had filmed it.

We watched again.

The creature had grabbed his cape.

Only his cape.

On the screen, one corner curled around its wrist.

There wasn't any wind.

### 0:47–1:05 — Things we hadn't noticed

Sol hadn't come outside at lunch all week.

Yesterday, he snapped at a little kid who stepped on his cape.

And in our class photo, everyone was standing still.

Except the red fabric reaching toward Bea.

We went to his office while he was at assembly.

### 1:05–1:23 — The instruction

Inside his desk was the guardian's delivery form.

Sol had ordered it himself.

Under special instructions, he'd written:

“If I tell you to leave, do not obey me.”

There was another sentence underneath.

“Get it off before I hurt someone.”

Behind us, the door clicked shut.

### 1:23–1:42 — Who is speaking?

Sol stood in the doorway.

His cape slid across the handle.

“You children should be in assembly.”

Sam backed into the desk.

Bea pulled the blinds open.

Sunlight struck the cape. It recoiled.

Sol grabbed the doorframe.

For a second, his voice sounded like him again.

“The gym. Go.”

### 1:42–2:02 — The chase

We ran.

Sam slammed the corridor door behind us.

A strip of red caught between the door and its frame.

Sol stopped moving.

“Don't open it!” he shouted.

The fabric pulled free.

His hand slammed through the door window.

We ran faster.

### 2:02–2:25 — The trap

Bea unlocked the gym cage.

The guardian crawled out, sniffed her, and shoved her behind it.

Sol floated through the gym doors.

I stood beneath the folding partition.

“Over here.”

The cape spread across the room.

Sam reached the partition controls.

I waited until the red edge crossed the floor track.

“Now!”

### 2:25–2:45 — The real monster

The partition clamped shut on the fabric.

Sol fell onto the gym mat.

The cape kept pulling without him.

The guardian pounced.

We heard fabric tear. Then nothing.

On Monday, Sol walked through the gate in an ordinary jacket.

The monster sniffed him.

Sol held very still.

Then it rolled onto its back.

He scratched its belly.

Bea held out her sandwich.

This time, it waited until she took the cheese out.

## Staging and clues

- Seed the cape's independent movement before the office reveal. The principal is
  a victim resisting possession, not secretly a monster with an unexplained change
  of heart. Sunlight buys seconds; it does not defeat the parasite.
- Make the control mechanism visible: door pinches cape → Sol briefly regains
  himself; gym partition repeats the same mechanism at a larger scale.
- Only imply impact; no broken skin, blood, or distressing transformation.
- Required assets: gate, classroom photo, office/blinds/desk/form, corridor door,
  gym cage, folding partition, crash mat, sandwich, six-eyed guardian, living cape.
- The mystery and rescue conclude in this reel. End with cautious friendship,
  not an unrelated final-frame monster added solely to force a sequel.
