# My Power Stops When You Look at Me

Production draft 1. Target: 2:30. Complete episode; hopeful superhero mystery.
Cast: Eli (student), Dad (school caretaker), Lens (masked villain), two classmates.
Power contract: Eli can move at super speed only outside direct sight or a live
camera view. Recordings watched later do not count. Ordinary movement is unaffected.
Dad has the same limitation but has trained to produce brief bursts while watched.

## Script

### 0:00–0:14 — The worst audition

At superhero tryouts, Maya lifts a bench. Noah floats above it.

I stand on the starting line.

“Show us,” the coach says.

Everyone watches.

Nothing happens.

My power only works when nobody can see me.

That's a difficult thing to prove in front of an audience.

### 0:14–0:29 — Someone already knows

Dad is mopping outside the gym when I come out.

“How'd it go?”

I show him the rejection slip.

He folds it. Puts it in his pocket.

“Walk home with me?”

I tell him I want to be alone.

He starts to answer, then lets me go.

Behind me, Dad covers the broken security camera with his cleaning cloth.

### 0:29–0:49 — The attack

The courtyard gate folds inward.

A man in a mirrored helmet walks through it.

He's looking for the school's power core.

He sends a bench sliding across the courtyard. It pins Noah's leg.

Everyone runs toward the exit. Lens turns to stop them.

For half a second, nobody is looking at Noah.

I move.

When Lens turns back, the bench is empty.

### 0:49–1:07 — A rescue he cannot explain

Maya is trapped on the stairs.

I knock over a bin. Lens looks toward the sound.

Maya disappears behind a pillar—and lands beside Noah at the gate.

Lens looks at the empty stairs.

Then at the pillar.

Then straight at me.

“There you are.”

### 1:07–1:27 — No blind spots

I throw dust in his face.

Two little lights blink on his helmet.

“Cameras,” he says. “You didn't think I'd come unprepared?”

I hit the courtyard light switch from behind a pillar.

His lenses turn green.

Night vision.

Across the yard, the damaged stair landing starts to sink.

One student is still on it.

### 1:27–1:46 — The caretaker

Dad steps out with his mop.

“School's closed.”

Lens barely turns his head.

Dad moves three metres in a blink.

Then drops to one knee.

His hand is shaking.

I stare at him.

“You too?”

“Since I was your age.”

Lens swings both cameras toward him.

### 1:46–2:10 — One useful step

Dad gets up. Lens watches him. So do I.

Another impossible step.

Dad cries out and catches himself on the mop.

He spent years learning to do what I couldn't do for a second.

He isn't trying to reach Lens.

He's moving between us.

“Eli,” he says. “Stop watching me.”

I look toward the trapped student.

Dad lifts his wide work coat across the helmet's lenses.

The landing breaks.

### 2:10–2:30 — The rescue and the lesson

Behind the coat, I move.

One burst: catch the student, carry her through the exit, loop behind Lens,
and pull the emergency shutter release.

The steel gate drops between Lens and the evacuated school. Dad is on our side.

Later, Dad sits on a bench with an ice pack on his knee.

“You could've told me.”

He hands me the folded rejection slip.

On the back, he's drawn a starting line.

“Try again?”

He looks directly at me.

I take one ordinary step.

“That's where I started,” he says.

## Staging and clues

- Show Dad covering a lens before the attack; it becomes evidence, not an unexplained
  new ability. His watched bursts are visibly painful and extremely limited.
- Show helmet cameras and night-vision lenses clearly. Dad's coat blocks every
  active lens together. Never show a successful unseen burst in an unobstructed
  witness view. Film cameras are non-diegetic and do not count as observers.
- Falling set pieces are controlled animation, not random physics. No injury detail.
- Required assets: school courtyard, gym entrance, stairs/landing, gate/shutter,
  bench, bin/dust, maintenance mop/coat, helmet lenses, power-core case, slip.
- Resolve the rescue and family reveal. The villain is contained, not inexplicably
  defeated by speed while cameras are still watching.
