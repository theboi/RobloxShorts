## Working editorial approach

- Start with the desired emotional change, then find an action that can express it.
- Establish a small object early and change what it means by the ending.
- Give the protagonist a choice that makes the emotional resolution visible.
- Prefer a complete payoff within a reel. For longer stories, each installment
  should resolve a local question before opening a specific consequential one.
- Separate verified source details, thematic interpretation, and original invention.
  Do not attribute invented scenes or conclusions to the reference authors.

For In Pencil, the doorway, height marks, suitcase, and sticker payoff are our
own scene design. The reference is the general movement from exclusion toward
belonging, rather than a condensed retelling of a fanfic.
